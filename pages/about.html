ABOUT / AYUSH ROY

I'm Ayush Roy.
Graphic & Communication Design.

Turning ideas and emotions into clean, concept-driven visuals with a playful edge.

My approach

I like turning ideas and emotions into clear, concept-driven visuals. My process starts with understanding the message, then exploring composition, visual style, and details to create work that feels thoughtful, expressive, and easy to connect with.

What I focus on

Illustration, branding, typography, and layout design. I’m interested in how colour, type, imagery, and composition work together to give each design its own personality while keeping the message clear.
